The NolMart Standard

Why Our Perfumes Last While Others Fade

Most commercial sprays sold in retail are Eau de Toilette (EDT) diluted with 85% water and alcohol. NolMart Scents operates on a strict artisanal standard.

💧

60% Oil vs 15% Standard

We blend 6.0ml pure concentrated fragrance oil per 10ml bottle (and 18ml per 30ml bottle). That is 4x higher oil density than standard mall perfumes.

🧪

3-Drop Fixative Anchor

Every bottle is infused with 3 precise drops of specialized molecular fixative that binds the fragrance notes to your skin oils, preventing premature evaporation.

⏳

Artisanal 15-Min Maturation

Unlike rushed mass production, each signature batch is hand-blended and rested for up to 15 minutes allowing top, heart, and base notes to marry seamlessly.
