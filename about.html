The NolMart Scents Standard

Born from a refusal to accept diluted perfumes that evaporate after 2 hours. We set out to formulate scents that command presence from morning to midnight.

1. The 60/40 Ratio: Extrait de Parfum Concentration

In modern perfumery, standard retail fragrances fall into low-concentration tiers: Eau de Cologne (3–5% oil) and Eau de Toilette (10–15% oil). Even Eau de Parfum (EDP) rarely exceeds 18% oil. The rest is water and alcohol carrier.

At NolMart Scents, every single bottle is compounded using our strict 60/40 base formula:

  • 60% Pure Essential Fragrance Oil: The active scent soul, imported and rigorously quality-tested.
  • 40% Perfumer's High-Grade Ethanol: The pure evaporation vehicle ensuring an initial radiant lift without chemical alcohol burn.
  • 3 Drops Molecular Fixative: Added last to each individual bottle, anchoring volatile fragrance molecules to human skin oils.
